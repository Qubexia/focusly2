## 🇬🇧 English (en-US)

### App name

```
Zakerly: Study Focus Timer
```

### Short description

```
Plan your study, focus with Pomodoro, and turn your notes into AI flashcards.
```

### Full description

```
Zakerly is a study app that stays with you from the moment you open the book until you finish the syllabus.

Instead of drifting between distractions, Zakerly turns studying into a clear plan, measured focus sessions, and real numbers that show whether you are actually making progress.

⏱️ A Pomodoro timer that works the way you study
Start a focus session, pause it when life interrupts, and resume from the same point. Every session is recorded with its duration and subject, so you can see today's work and your full history.

📚 Organise every subject and chapter
Add your subjects, split them into chapters, and keep your tasks under the right one. Everything sits in its place and connects back to your study sessions.

🗓️ A study schedule that repeats itself
Build your timetable once and let it recur — daily, weekly, or on the days you choose. View it as a calendar and tick off each session as you complete it.

🤖 AI study assistant
Upload a PDF or photograph pages of your handwritten notes, and Zakerly reads them and produces:
• a structured summary of the material
• flashcards to test yourself with
• practice questions on the same content
You choose the output language and how detailed you want it.

📊 Analytics that tell you the truth
• A summary of your study time
• Time split per subject, so you can see what you have been avoiding
• A heatmap of your active days
• A performance score calculated from your actual sessions

🔥 Streaks and points that keep you going
Every day you study builds your streak. Consistency is what separates one student from another, and Zakerly keeps it visible.

🔔 Reminders that arrive on time
Study reminders and streak alerts, with every category switchable on or off individually in Settings.

🎯 Focus mode
Turn it on during a session to cut distractions and finish what you sat down to do.

☁️ Your account travels with you
Sign in with Google or email, and your data stays in sync across your devices.

—

Subscription
Zakerly is free to use, with an optional paid subscription that unlocks the AI features and advanced tools. Payments are processed securely by Paymob, and we never store your card details.

Your privacy
We do not sell your data, and we do not read your notes to target ads at you. Read the full privacy policy here:
https://zakerlyai.tech/privacy

Questions or problems? Write to support@focaly.app and we will get back to you.

Start with one subject and one session today. The rest follows.
```
